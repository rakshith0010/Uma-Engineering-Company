About UMA Engineering Industries

UMA Engineering Industries is an established manufacturing business based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, with a rich history dating back to **1982 when it was founded by Mr. C. Angappan Pillai**.

Operating under our trusted flagship brands **UMA Pumps** and **UMA Motors**, we engineer and supply robust industrial and agricultural pumping machinery built for long-term reliability.
